# Runic Synthesis app icon

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. `master.png` is the original generated artwork; web and Android exports are produced by `scripts/prepare-app-icons.py` using Pillow. Android adaptive exports add padding to protect the emblem when launcher masks crop the image. The original full-bleed artwork is used for the web and legacy launcher icons.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: final production square launcher app icon for RUNIC SYNTHESIS, a dark fantasy five-element rune merge puzzle game. Create ONE polished premium hand-painted fantasy game app icon, 1024x1024 square. Full bleed nearly uniform midnight navy background #080f20, no rounded corners, no external frame or mockup. Subject: a single bold faceted luminous cyan rune crystal, angular vaguely R-shaped magical sigil engraved in its luminous face, cradled by a thick antique gold pentagonal alchemical setting. Five large restrained jewel facets integrated into the setting represent fire ember red, water cyan blue, wood emerald green, earth amber gold, lightning violet. The central crystal is the dominant clear silhouette, not five separate floating objects. Match saturated painterly fantasy inventory art with sharp white-gold highlights and dimensional polished facets. High contrast and visually simple, easy to recognize at 32px, no tiny ornaments, no text, no lettering, no words, no weapon, no books, no UI. CRITICAL COMPOSITION: all meaningful artwork including glow must remain within the central 60 percent width and height of the full square, leaving a generous dark navy safety margin on every side so Android circular cropping is safe. Main emblem vertically centered, symmetrical visual weight, glowing turquoise center with warm gold structural edges. Background dark navy reaches every edge, no outside drop shadow, no watermark.
